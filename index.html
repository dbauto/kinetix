01Recruitment Operations Platform

Give recruiters one place to manage candidate movement and review.

Applicant, job-opening, facility, follow-up and reporting activity were brought into one operating platform. Recruiters can trace the recruitment lifecycle, review structured resume analysis and rediscover past applicants for future opportunities.

Value for the team
  • Less time reconciling information across separate recruitment processes.
  • Clearer visibility into each applicant's recruitment history and current work.
  • Structured resume analysis available directly where recruiters review candidates.

02Enterprise Operations Workspace

Give teams one workspace for work, ownership and leadership visibility.

Projects, tasks, priorities, escalations, approvals, documents and reporting were organized into a governed company workspace. Team members can focus on the work they own while leadership can see the wider portfolio and what needs attention.

Value for the team
  • Every task stays connected to a project and an accountable owner.
  • Overdue work, priorities and escalations are easier to see and act on.

03SPX OCR Automated Encoder System

Turn incoming documents into usable records instead of repetitive encoding.

Daily collection information arriving through images and workbooks was turned into a repeatable processing flow. Documents are read, checked and organized into structured records, while uncertain documents stay visible for human review.

Value for the team
  • Reduce repetitive re-encoding of seller, route, driver and collection information.
  • Keep unclear or incomplete documents visible instead of silently accepting bad data.
  • Create repeatable operational and reconciliation reports from approved records.

04Enterprise Zoho CRM Engineering & Migration

Move large recruitment datasets without losing the information that makes them useful.

A large migration program was structured so resume files, candidate identity, facility information, licenses and review confidence stayed traceable as records moved into the CRM. The process also made exceptions and reconciliation visible before acceptance.

Value for the business
  • Preserve links between source files and the CRM records they belong to.
  • Flag lower-confidence records for review instead of treating every row as equally safe.
  • Reconcile and verify migrated information before the transition is considered complete.
